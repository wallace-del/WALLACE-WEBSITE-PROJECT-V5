Privacy

Simple portfolio, minimal data handling.

WALLACE.DEV is a personal portfolio with a server-backed contact form and portfolio assistant.

Contact messages

When you submit the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you provide are sent to the WALLACE.DEV backend and stored in its database so Wallace can review and respond. An email notification is also sent to Wallace. Messages can be deleted from the authenticated admin dashboard.

Wallace AI Assistant

Questions sent to Wallace AI are processed by the WALLACE.DEV backend to generate answers from verified portfolio information. The current application does not save assistant questions as portfolio records.

Other services

If analytics or additional third-party services are added later, this page should be updated before those services are treated as production features.
